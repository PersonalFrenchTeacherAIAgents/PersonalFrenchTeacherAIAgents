# Personal French Teacher AI Agents

**Specialised AI Learning Systems · Professional Learning · Sports AI · Local-First**

Personal French Teacher began in 2004 as a highly personalised French-language and intercultural training activity for international learners, executives and specialised professional audiences.

Today, that expertise has evolved into a portfolio of **specialised AI learning agents** designed as structured educational systems rather than generic chatbots.

## Design principles

- Structured learning rather than open-ended chat alone
- Specialised professional scenarios and terminology
- Exercises, correction workflows and learner progression
- Local-first and privacy-oriented configurations
- Offline-compatible options depending on product configuration
- BYOK options where applicable
- Perpetual licence for the purchased software version
- Optional third-party AI/API services may carry separate provider costs
